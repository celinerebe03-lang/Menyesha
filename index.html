What is Menyesha?

A place to speak up and take part

Menyesha is designed to connect residents' observations and ideas with the people responsible for reviewing community concerns. It helps bring similar reports together and makes it easier to follow updates.

Steps to report your problem

Clear, accurate information makes a report more useful.

1

Describe what you noticed

Explain the problem in simple, respectful words. Say when you noticed it and how it affects people, if you know.

2

Check for the same problem

Look through community reports. If the same issue is already listed, choose “I have the same problem” instead of repeating the report.

3

Give a useful location

Provide the district, sector, cell, village, or nearby landmark. Avoid posting your exact home address publicly.

4

Add evidence if safe

You may add a photo or other helpful details. Do not put yourself at risk to collect evidence or share another person's private information.

5

Review and submit

Check that your details are correct. Share your name or phone number only if needed and through a secure private form.

6

Track updates

Return to the report to check whether it is under review, being handled, or marked as resolved.

Respect and safety

Your privacy matters

Community reports should help people understand an issue without exposing the person who submitted it.

Public information

Problem description, general location, status, and the number of people who confirm the issue.

Private information

Names, phone numbers, account details, and exact home addresses should not appear on public report cards.

Authorized access

A secure live system should use sign-in, role-based permissions, and server-side checks before staff can view private details.

This HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype does not provide real account security, secure storage, or private leader access. Those protections require a secure backend and database.

About Menyesha

Every voice can contribute.

Menyesha is a concept for community reporting and idea sharing. It aims to help residents explain local needs, avoid repeated reports, and give responsible organizations clearer information to review. It does not replace emergency services or guarantee that every submission will be acted upon.
